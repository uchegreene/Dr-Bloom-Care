Daily AI tips require medical-team approval before publication. Approved tips should publish at 9:00 a.m. West Africa Time (Nigeria), not the creator's local time.

**Why:** The owner explicitly selected medical review and Nigeria's morning schedule for premium-member health education.

**How to apply:** Separate pregnancy and cycle audiences using active membership entitlements. Label content as AI-generated education and identify actual human reviewers separately; never give an AI author a verified professional badge. Generate shared audience content without sending members' private health logs or messages to the model. Keep diagnosis, prescriptions and personalised treatment with qualified professionals. Notify members only about tips that have actually been approved and published.
