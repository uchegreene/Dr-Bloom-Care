---
name: Branding continuity
description: Keep existing accounts and health data intact when renaming the app.
---

Treat app-name changes as display rebrands within the existing product, not new app identities.

**Why:** The owner requested a name change, not a new application or reset of accounts and medical logs.

**How to apply:** Update visible copy, native display settings and artifact title. Preserve existing storage keys, database identifiers, authentication tenant, routes and deep-link scheme unless a separately authorized migration is necessary. Do not rename individual professional profiles or rewrite existing community content merely because the app brand changed.
