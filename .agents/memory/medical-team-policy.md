Paid members may tag verified medical profiles on public community posts and send private topic-based DMs to a selected profile, regardless of cycle/pregnancy mode. Free members remain read-only.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested these communication benefits for paid users.

**How to apply:** Check current paid entitlement on member writes and private discussion access; mode switching must not affect communication access.

Named professionals work as an employed care team with a shared medical-admin inbox. A question addressed to a general profile such as Dr Bloom can be answered by an appropriate specialist, who replies under their own named, verified profile.

**Why:** The user chose a shared inbox and explained that employed doctors, gynecologists, midwives and other specialists each own a profile while working together.

**How to apply:** Link professional identities through explicit administrator authorization. Medical-team access does not grant billing/admin powers. Attribute replies to the actual responding profile, prevent unauthorized profile impersonation, and clearly tell members that authorized team members share access to DMs. Do not fabricate clinician responses.

Medical staff such as doctors and gynecologists have an assigned professional profile name, separate from their personal account details.

**Why:** The owner explicitly specified that assigned clinical staff will have an assigned profile name.

**How to apply:** Show the assigned name in staff setup and use the linked professional identity for medical advice and replies. Personal-name edits must not rename that public identity or allow impersonating a different professional.
