---
name: Pregnancy reminder continuity and privacy
description: Why existing medical alerts survive mode/billing changes and keep lock-screen content generic.
---

Creating or editing pregnancy appointments and medicine schedules requires the matching active pregnancy plan and pregnancy mode. Existing schedules should not be silently cancelled solely because the user changes viewing mode or the membership expires.

**Why:** A view or billing change is not a medical instruction to stop taking medicine or miss a previously booked appointment.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing alert schedules while the same account stays signed in. Explicit deletion, notification permission changes and signing out/account switching are distinct reasons to remove or suspend phone alerts. Keep new health logging subject to the membership policy.

Keep medicine names, clinic details and personal notes out of lock-screen notification content; show a generic care reminder instead.

**Why:** Bloom contains sensitive women's health information and phones may be shared or visible to others.

**How to apply:** Associate alerts with their owning account internally, cancel old-account alerts on account switches, and show detailed reminder records only within the account-scoped app.
